Light Bonds

Same equation as gravity. Each electron is pulled toward every nucleus as Z/r² (like GM/r²) and pushed away from every other electron as 1/r². Nothing is drawn by hand: a bond is whatever orbit carries an electron around both atoms.

Shape and energy

Simulation of the model's rule, not quantum chemistry. Every pull and push uses one law, strength ÷ r² (softened very close in). Each electron keeps the energy it started with, the way a loop's closure fixes its energy (E·T = h); without that, a classical cloud of electrons slowly throws one out. Nuclei sit at the measured bond lengths; angles and bond energies listed are measured values for comparison.

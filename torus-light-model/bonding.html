Light Bonds

A charge is one figure-eight of light: two loops. A loop is one electron. An atom with a loop left unpaired can join it with another atom's unpaired loop; the joined pair is a bond, one loop around each nucleus.

Bond: a shared charge, one loop around each atom Unpaired loop: half a charge, waiting for a partner Lone pair: a charge with both loops on its own atom Inner shell: the full first shell, one charge

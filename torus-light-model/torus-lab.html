Geometry

The tube breathes outward from the energy loop, two breaths per loop (2mc²/h). At ε = √(4α/π) the breathing alone gives g − 2 = α/2π.
Both loops share one period. At 2 the charge sits on the torus rim and moves at c (at rest shape).

Moving loop: why the path gets longer

The light still travels at c (hypotenuse). Forward motion takes part of it, so less is left for going around, and the loop takes γ times longer to close. At v = c nothing is left: the loop never closes.
